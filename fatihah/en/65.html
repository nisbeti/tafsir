The path of guidance, did they choose it? We say no... and read His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾And as for Thamud, We guided them, but they preferred blindness over guidance, so a thunderbolt of the humiliating punishment seized them for what they used to earn (17)﴿ [Fussilat]. Thus, there are those who do not take the path of guidance by the choice that Allah has given them. If Allah, the Exalted, had wanted all of us to be guided, no one among His creation could have acted against His will. However, He, Exalted is He, created us as choosers so that we may come to Him out of love and desire, rather than being compelled to obey. What happens to those who followed the path of guidance and those who did not and opposed Allah's legal will in His creation?

Those who follow the path of guidance, Allah, Exalted is He, assists them in it and makes them fond of faith and piety, and makes them fond of obedience to Him.

And read His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾And those who are guided, He increases them in guidance and grants them their piety﴿ [Muhammad: 17]. This means that everyone who takes the path of guidance, Allah assists them in it and increases them in piety and love for the religion. As for those who, when guidance comes to them, turn away from the path of Allah and oppose it, then Allah, Exalted is He, abandons them and leaves them in their misguidance. And read His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾And whoever turns away from the remembrance of the Most Merciful, We appoint for him a devil, and he becomes his companion (36)﴿ [al-Zukhruf]. And Allah, Exalted is He, has clarified to us the deprived ones from the guidance of assistance in faith, and they are three, as He has made clear to us in the Noble Qur'an: ﴾That is because they preferred the worldly life over the Hereafter, and that Allah does not guide the disbelieving people (107)﴿ [al-Nahl], ﴾That is closer that they may give testimony in its proper form or fear that their oaths will be rejected after their oaths. And fear Allah and listen; and Allah does not guide the disobedient people (108)﴿ [al-Ma'idah], ﴾Have you not seen the one who argued with Abraham about his Lord because Allah had given him kingship? When Abraham said, "My Lord is He who gives life and causes death," he said, "I give life and cause death." Abraham said, "Indeed, Allah brings the sun from the east, so bring it from the west." So the one who disbelieved was overwhelmed. And Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people (258)﴿ [al-Baqarah]. Thus, those who are expelled from Allah's guidance in assistance with faith are the disbelievers, the disobedient, and the wrongdoing. (148)

The states of the transitive action of guidance in the language:

Dr. Fadil al-Samarrai said: The action of guidance (hidaya) in Arabic can be transitive by itself without a preposition, such as ﴾Guide us to the straight path﴿ (the verb is transitive by itself), and it can be transitive with "to" ﴾And indeed, you guide to a straight path (52)﴿ (Ash-Shura) ﴾And I will guide you to your Lord so that you may fear (19)﴿ (An-Nazi'at) and it can be transitive with "for" ﴾And they said, "Praise be to Allah, who has guided us to this, and we would not have been guided had it not been that Allah guided us"﴿ (Al-A'raf 43) ﴾Rather, Allah confers a favor upon you by guiding you to faith if you should be among the truthful (17)﴿ (Al-Hujurat)
