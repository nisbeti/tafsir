In its fullness of truth. This is the faith that Islam calls for, and it is not the faith that they refer to as the faith of the elderly; rather, it is the faith of the enlightened and certain one who has observed and reflected, then thought and pondered, and thus arrived at certainty in Allah, Exalted is He.

And although it is necessary for a person to use reason to arrive at faith in Allah, the Exalted, he cannot comprehend what is beyond his senses and beyond his intellect, because human reason is limited, and its power, no matter how elevated and developed, is confined by boundaries it cannot surpass. Therefore, it is limited in perception, and hence, reason must fall short of comprehending the essence of Allah, and it is incapable of grasping His reality, because Allah is beyond the universe, humanity, and life, and human reason cannot perceive the reality of what lies beyond humanity; thus, it is unable to comprehend the essence of Allah. (143)

After a person recognizes the existence of Allah, the Exalted, he finds himself in need of the path that leads him to Him, and the reason for which he was created, and the duty he must fulfill on earth, and it clarifies for him what he must do for the betterment of his affairs, and what will earn him the pleasure of his Creator and His reward, and what he must avoid, which incurs the wrath of his Creator and His punishment. Thus, there arose the need for the messengers, to clarify for people their connection with the Creator, Exalted is He, beyond the connection of creation and existence; that connection is the connection of commands and prohibitions, and the connection of the paths that establish life and ensure that a person reaches the ultimate goal and safety in this world and the Hereafter, and it clarifies for him matters of the unseen. All of this is the path that connects the servant to his Lord, Exalted is He, and it is upon a person to supplicate to Allah, the Exalted, to guide him to this path, so that he may reach the desired goal and be saved from the missteps and errors that lead to paths of deviation and misguidance.

﴾Guide us to the straight path﴿: This is a supplication, and there is no other supplication that is obligatory for a Muslim to recite besides this supplication, which the Muslim must say several times a day. This, in turn, indicates the importance of the request and this supplication, for it has its effects in this world and the Hereafter, and it indicates that a person cannot find guidance to the straight path by himself unless Allah, the Exalted, guides him to it. If people were left to themselves, each would go to his own sect and would not find guidance to the straight path.
