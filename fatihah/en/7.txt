They reconciled with him, and some of them said: "Distribute [the wealth]." But the one who performed the incantation said: "Do not do that until we go to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) and inform him of what happened, then we will see what he commands us." So they went to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) and mentioned it to him. He said: "And how do you know it is an incantation? You have succeeded; distribute [the wealth] and give me a share with you."(19)

(19) Bukhari, Medicine (5417), Muslim, Peace (2201), Tirmidhi, Medicine (2063), Abu Dawood, Transactions (3418), Ibn Majah, Trades (2156), Ahmad (3/50).
