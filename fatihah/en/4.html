The surah begins with the praise of Allah, then His glorification, followed by the declaration of dependence on Him, the submission before Him, the request for assistance from Him, and the seeking of guidance through Him. Thus, half of it is a gathering of praise, and the other half is a gathering of needs. It introduces people to their Lord, with His names and attributes, and informs them of the relationship between them and their Lord: ﴾You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help﴿. It guides them to the path of their Lord: the straight path, and it informs them about those who have followed the straight path of their Lord and those who have deviated from it.

The Opening (Al-Fatiha) is called the Great Qur'an because it encompasses all the sciences of the Qur'an, as it includes the praise of Allah, Exalted is He, with descriptions of His perfection and majesty, the command to worship and to be sincere in it, the acknowledgment of one’s inability to perform any of it except with His assistance, and the supplication to Him for guidance to the straight path and for the sufficiency of the conditions of the transgressors, as well as the clarification of the consequences for the deniers.

Al-Fatiha contains the concepts of monotheism (tawhid), worship, reliance (tawakkul), admonition, and reminder(9) and the methodologies of life, and it teaches the question, as it encompasses a range of meanings of the Qur'an from theoretical rulings and practical laws. The sciences include monotheism, attributes, prophecies, admonitions, parables, wisdom, and stories, while the rulings pertain either to the actions of the limbs, which are acts of worship and transactions, or to the actions of the hearts, meaning the intellect, which is the refinement of morals and the etiquette of the Sharia. All of these meanings are included in Al-Fatiha through indication, implication, or entailment. Thus, ﴾All praise is due to Allah﴿ encompasses all attributes of perfection for which Allah deserves to have praise confined to Him, based on what the phrase ﴾All praise is due to Allah﴿ indicates regarding the exclusivity of praise to Him. And ﴾Lord of the worlds﴿ encompasses all attributes of action and creation according to those who affirm them, and ﴾The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful﴿ encompasses the foundations of legislation that relate to mercy for the accountable beings, and ﴾Master of the Day of Judgment﴿ encompasses the conditions of the Day of Resurrection, and ﴾You alone we worship﴿ gathers the meaning of religion and law, while ﴾And You alone we ask for help﴿ gathers the meaning of sincerity to Allah in actions(10). In the surah, there is an explanation of the conduct of the straight path and insight into the ranks of the blessed and the abodes of the wretched, the former being inferred from the phrase ﴾Guide us﴿; and insight into the ranks of the blessed and the abodes of the wretched is derived from the phrase ﴾Those upon whom You have bestowed favor﴿, etc. In it, there is a promise and a warning, thus both are included(11). The parables and stories are intended for admonition, as well as supplication and praise and teaching the question. These are the general meanings of the Qur'anic text in summary, both in terms of correspondence and entailment.
