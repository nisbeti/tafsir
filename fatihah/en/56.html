Peace be upon him: "My community will never unite upon misguidance." Narrated by Ahmad. Allah has made Paradise for the guided and Hellfire for the misguided, meaning that Allah rewards the guided and punishes the misguided. The connection of reward or punishment to guidance and misguidance indicates that guidance and misguidance are actions of the human being and not from Allah. If they were from Allah, He would not reward for guidance and punish for misguidance, as this would attribute injustice to Allah, the Exalted. When He punishes someone whom He Himself has misguided, He would be unjust to him, and Exalted is Allah above that. He, the Exalted, said: ﴾And your Lord is not unjust to the servants (46)﴿ and He said: ﴾And I am not unjust to the servants (29)﴿.(144)

Guidance and misguidance between compulsion and choice

As for guidance and misguidance, there is no doubt that it is the human being who is guided and who goes astray. If he is guided, he has attained guidance by himself through his intellect and thinking, in his striving and actions. He is guided by choice and goes astray by choice, without any compulsion, just like any action among the actions that he controls. Thus, guidance and misguidance are not among the actions that occur upon a person without his control, such as the actions that belong to what we call the system of existence, nor are they among the actions that are beyond the capacity of the human being to avert, nor among the actions necessitated by the system of existence.(145) Therefore, they are not from the sphere that controls him, but rather from the sphere that he controls.

Guidance and misguidance are not like the beating of the heart, the color of the eyes, the shape of the head, and similar things that are part of the system of existence. They are not like a sleeping person falling from a roof, or a careless person slipping on something smooth, nor like the disbelief of a weak person threatened with death by a strong one, nor anything similar that is beyond the human being's capacity to avert. Likewise, they are not like the wings of a bird, or the legs of a walker, or light for the eyes, or anything similar that is necessitated by the system of existence. Rather, they are like eating by a human being, like traveling, walking, buying and selling, fasting and praying, and other actions that fall within the sphere that the human being controls, where he can, by his own choice, engage in these actions or refrain from them.

Thus, guidance and misguidance are from the human being himself; they are actions of his, like all his voluntary actions.

(145) Among the actions that occur in the sphere that the human being does not control is that he is born on a specific date, with a certain skin color, height, and that he dies on a specific date in a specific place, or that a person shoots a bird and it unintentionally rebounds and kills him, and similar actions that the human being cannot avert. These actions fall within the realm of destiny and divine decree, for which those upon whom these actions occur are neither rewarded nor punished. As for the actions that occur in the sphere that he controls, such as praying or not praying, selling or not selling, these are actions that the human being controls and is rewarded for or punished for, as they are outside the realm of destiny and divine decree. Although everything that occurs is known by Allah and recorded in the Preserved Tablet, there is no compulsion in the sphere that the human being controls.
