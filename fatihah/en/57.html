This is the reality of guidance and misguidance.

The relationship of guidance and misguidance to humans in the Qur'an:

Many verses of the Qur'an explicitly state that guidance and misguidance are actions of human beings, and they clearly attribute guidance and misguidance to the servant. Allah, Exalted is He, says: ﴾Say, "O mankind, the truth has come to you from your Lord. So whoever is guided is only guided for himself, and whoever goes astray only goes astray against it. And I am not a guardian over you."﴿ (Yunus 108) ﴾Indeed, We have sent down to you the Book for mankind in truth. So whoever is guided, it is for his own soul, and whoever goes astray, only goes astray against it. And you are not a guardian over them.﴿ (39) (Az-Zumar 41), ﴾Indeed, those who go astray from the path of Allah will have a severe punishment.﴿ (Sad 26), ﴾It will not harm you if those who go astray do not believe, as long as you have been guided.﴿ (Al-Ma'idah 105) ﴾And they are the rightly guided.﴿ (157) (Al-Baqarah), ﴾Say, "If I go astray, I only go astray to the detriment of my own soul. And if I am guided, it is by what my Lord has revealed to me. Indeed, He is Hearing and Near."﴿ (34) (Saba 50), ﴾And whoever exchanges disbelief for faith has certainly gone astray from the soundness of the path.﴿ (18) (Al-Baqarah) ﴾And whoever associates others with Allah has certainly gone far astray.﴿ (119) (An-Nisa) ﴾And whoever disbelieves in Allah and His angels and His Books and His messengers and the Last Day has certainly gone far astray.﴿ (136) (An-Nisa) ﴾And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly gone astray into clear error.﴿ (36) (Al-Ahzab), ﴾So if they submit, they have been rightly guided; but if they turn away, then upon you is only the duty of notification.﴿ (Al-Imran 20), ﴾And if you obey him, you will be guided. And there is upon the Messenger only the duty of clear notification.﴿ (54) (An-Nur).

These verses are explicit in indicating that guidance is attributed to the servant, and that he is the one who is guided, and he is the one who goes astray. This clearly means that it is the human being who is guided, and it is he who has gone astray.

And also, indeed Allah, Exalted is He, holds people accountable; He rewards the guided and punishes the misguided, and He has arranged the reckoning based on a person's deeds. He, Exalted is He, said: ﴾Whoever does good, it is for his own soul; and whoever does evil, it is against it; and your Lord is not unjust to the servants﴿ (41) (Fussilat), ﴾So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it (7) and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it (8)﴿ (Al-Zalzalah), ﴾And whoever does righteous deeds while he is a believer, he will not fear injustice or deprivation (112)﴿ (Ta-Ha), ﴾Whoever does evil will be recompensed for it﴿ (An-Nisa 123), ﴾Allah has promised the hypocrites and the hypocritical women and the disbelievers the fire of Hell, abiding therein﴿ (At-Tawbah 68). These verses are explicit in indicating that Allah holds people accountable for guidance and misguidance, and it would be unjust if He compelled someone to do something and then held them accountable for it; Allah is far above that, Exalted is He. This is because the reckoning is for everything they did not do themselves, and because punishing individuals by Allah for an act they performed, not for what others did, is undoubtedly injustice, and Allah is far above that, Exalted is He. For this reason, it is the human being who is guided and the one who goes astray, and holding him accountable for that is just, because it is accountability for an act he himself performed, and Allah's punishment of the misguided is just, because it is punishment for their own actions.
