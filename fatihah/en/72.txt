The descriptions of the misguided Christians, as He said: ﴾Indeed, they have gone astray before, and they have led many astray, and they have gone astray from the straight path﴿ [Al-Ma'idah: 77].
It is recommended for one who recites Al-Fatiha to say afterwards: Amen [like: Yas], and it is said: Amen, with shortening as well [like: Yamin], and its meaning is: O Allah, respond. The evidence for this is what was narrated by Imam Ahmad, Abu Dawood, and al-Tirmidhi from Wail ibn Hujr, who said: I heard the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) recite: ﴾Not the path of those upon whom is Your wrath, nor of those who are astray﴿ and he said: "Amen," raising his voice with it. And in Abu Dawood: he raised his voice with it, and al-Tirmidhi said: This is a good hadith. It was narrated from Ali, Ibn Mas'ud, and others.
And from Abu Huraira, he said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) would say "Amen" when he recited ﴾Not the path of those upon whom is Your wrath, nor of those who are astray﴿ until those in the first row could hear him. Narrated by Abu Dawood and Ibn Majah, and he added: the mosque would echo with it, and al-Daraqutni said: This chain is good.
And from Bilal, he said: O Messenger of Allah, do not precede me with Amen. Narrated by Abu Dawood.
And from Anas, he said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "I was given Amen in prayer and when making supplication, no one was given it before me except that he was Moses. Moses would supplicate, and Aaron would say Amen, so conclude your supplication with Amen, for Allah will respond to you."
I said: And from here, some have derived evidence from this noble verse, which is His saying: ﴾And Moses said: Our Lord, indeed You have given Pharaoh and his people adornment and wealth in the worldly life, Our Lord, that they may lead [others] astray from Your path. Our Lord, obliterate their wealth and harden their hearts so they will not believe until they see the painful punishment. * Allah said: Your supplication has been answered, so remain steadfast and do not follow the path of those who do not know﴿ [Yunus: 88, 89]. He mentioned the supplication of Moses alone, and from the context of the speech, it indicates that Aaron said Amen, thus he is in the position of one who supplicates, due to His saying: ﴾Your supplication has been answered﴿ [Yunus: 89]. This indicates that whoever says Amen to a supplication is as if he has said it; hence, it was said that the follower (ma'mum) does not recite because his saying Amen after the recitation of Al-Fatiha is equivalent to reciting it; and thus it came in the hadith: "Whoever has an imam, the recitation of the imam is his recitation." And Bilal would say: Do not precede me with Amen. This indicates that the follower has no recitation in the audible prayers (155), and Allah knows best.
For this reason, Ibn Marduyah said: Ahmad ibn al-Hasan narrated to us, Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Salam narrated to us, Ishaq ibn Ibrahim narrated to us, Jarir narrated from Lyth ibn Abi Sulaim, from Ka'b, from Abu Huraira, who said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "When the imam says: ﴾Not the path of those upon whom is Your wrath, nor of those who are astray﴿ and then says: Amen, the Amen of the people of the earth coincides with the Amen of the inhabitants of the heavens, and Allah forgives the servant for what has preceded of his sins. And the example of one who does not say: Amen is like a man who went out to fight with a group, and they drew lots, and their arrows came out, but he did not come out.
