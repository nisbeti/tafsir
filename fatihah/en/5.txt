Prophethood is what is intended by His saying, Exalted is He, ﴾You have bestowed favor upon them﴿, and the Hereafter is alluded to by His saying, Exalted is He, ﴾Master of the Day of Judgment﴿. The second is the science of branches, and its foundation is acts of worship, which is what is meant by His saying ﴾You alone we worship﴿. These are physical and financial acts, such as prayer and zakat, which are dependent on matters of livelihood, such as transactions and marriages, and they must be governed (12). Thus, the branches are established upon the roots. The third is the science by which perfection is attained, which is the science of ethics, and its highest goal is reaching the Divine Presence, and following the straight path in the ranks of those high stations. This is indicated by His saying ﴾You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path﴿. The fourth is the science of stories and news about the past nations, both the happy and the wretched, and what is connected to them regarding promise and warning, which is what is meant by His saying, Exalted is He, ﴾You have bestowed favor upon them, not of those who have earned Your anger, nor of those who have gone astray﴿ (13). We benefit from His saying, ﴾Guide us to the straight path﴿, as it clarifies the methodologies of life that make life upright.

(12) This wonderful insight from the scholar al-Alusi, may Allah have mercy on him, connects the righteousness of livelihood with the necessity of governance, and that the righteousness of acts of worship and transactions cannot be achieved except through a state that organizes the interests of the people. He made it one of the main branches of knowledge that he derived from Surah Al-Fatiha, indicating his profound understanding, may Allah have mercy on him.
