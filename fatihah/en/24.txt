And Imam Ahmad narrated in his Musnad: Abdullah narrated to us, my father narrated to us, Yazid ibn Harun informed us, from Hisham al-Dastawai, from Yahya ibn Abi Kathir, from Ibrahim ibn Abdullah ibn Qaridh that his father informed him: “He entered upon Abdur-Rahman ibn Awf while he was ill, and Abdur-Rahman said to him: Your kinship is a mercy, for the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: The Exalted said: I am the Most Merciful (al-Rahman), I created the womb (rahim) and I derived its name from My name(75), so whoever maintains it, I maintain him, and whoever severs it, I sever him.”(76)
Ibn Faris said: The letters r, h, and m have one root that indicates tenderness, compassion, and mercy. It is said from this: He had mercy on him (rahimahu), if he was tender towards him and showed kindness to him. Mercy (rahma), compassion (marhama), and kindness (rahma) are synonymous. The womb (rahim) is the relationship of kinship, and it is named the womb of the female because from it comes that which is to be shown mercy and tenderness towards, from offspring.(77)
Ibn Manzur said in Lisan al-Arab: And Allah, the Most Merciful (al-Rahman), the Most Compassionate (al-Rahim), the first attribute is built on the pattern of fa'lan because its meaning is abundance, as His mercy encompasses all things and He is the Most Merciful of the merciful. As for the Most Compassionate (al-Rahim), it is mentioned after the Most Merciful (al-Rahman) because the Most Merciful is exclusive to Allah, the Exalted, while the Most Compassionate can be for others.(78)

(76) Maintaining kinship (silat al-arham) is a metaphor for showing kindness to those closest to you by blood and marriage, being compassionate towards them, being gentle with them, and taking care of their affairs, even if they are distant or do wrong. Severing kinship means cutting off all of that. Al-Nawawi said in his explanation of Muslim: The scholars said: The essence of maintaining kinship is compassion and mercy. In the hadith of Abu Huraira, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: {Indeed, Allah created creation, and when He finished with them, the womb stood up and said: This is the place of the one seeking refuge from severance. He said: Yes. Do you not wish that I maintain whoever maintains you and sever whoever severs you? She said: Yes. He said: That is for you.} There are many hadiths regarding those who sever kinship: from Abu Muhammad Jubair ibn Mut'im (may Allah be pleased with him) that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: “The severer will not enter Paradise.” It is agreed upon. Al-Bukhari reported in his Sahih that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: “The one who maintains kinship is not the one who merely reciprocates, but the one who, when his kinship is severed, maintains it.” And from Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her) that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: “The womb is suspended by the Throne, saying: Whoever maintains me, Allah maintains him, and whoever severs me, Allah severs him.” It is agreed upon. There are great wisdoms in maintaining kinship, the most important of which is expressed in the hadith of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him): {Whoever wishes for his provision to be expanded, or his lifespan to be extended, let him maintain his kinship.} Among the many benefits that the jurists have pointed out: May Allah be pleased with them; because He commanded maintaining kinship and bringing joy to relatives. Among them is the increase of nobility, and the increase of reward after death; because they pray for him after his death whenever they remember his kindness. Maintaining kinship can be achieved through many means, including: visiting, assisting, fulfilling needs, and greeting, as He (peace and blessings be upon him) said: {Maintain your kinship even if it is just with a greeting.} Likewise, giving money to relatives is considered a means of maintaining kinship; as He (peace and blessings be upon him) said: {Charity to the poor is charity, and to the one with kinship is two: charity and maintaining kinship.} And what is meant by maintaining kinship is not to maintain them if they maintain you; because this is reciprocation, but rather to maintain them even if they sever you. Al-Bukhari and others narrated {The one who maintains kinship is not the one who reciprocates, but the one who maintains kinship when it is severed.} See the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia and the book "From the Components of Islamic Psychology" published by Hizb al-Tahrir.
