They think that their ability to establish the rules of polytheism and to declare things forbidden and permissible is nothing but that Allah has created in them the capacity for that. They believe that when He does not restrain their actions, He must be pleased with what they have done, and that if He were not pleased with it, He would not be unable to take away their ability. They think that Allah is concerned about their wrongful actions regarding what He has created them upon, and if it were as they imagine, then falsehood and truth would be one and the same.

The reason for this deviation affecting the people of misguidance among the nations, which appears in the minds of some ordinary Muslims as they excuse themselves for sins and crimes by saying: "It is the command of Allah" or "It is written with Allah" or something similar, is ignorance of the fact that Allah's wisdom in establishing the system of this world necessitated that He place a veil between His actions regarding the conditions of the creatures and their actions regarding their own conditions according to their will. This veil is the law of the connection of causes to their effects, and the connection of the states of existents in this world to one another. This is the source of the obligation that indicates what Allah is pleased with and what He is not pleased with. Allah established the system of this world with wisdom, making its foundation the management of things according to their own natures based on the powers He has endowed in the existents so that they strive for what they were created for. He has also granted humanity the distinction of having a mind that enables them to change their conditions according to their needs, and He has placed in their minds means to discern good from evil, just as He has appointed callers to good to alert them to it if they fall into heedlessness or are veiled by desire. If they do not restrain their waywardness, they have betrayed the essence of their intellect by suppressing it.

Thus, the confusion of the people of misguidance between the will of the servants and the will of Allah has become evident. Therefore, Allah refuted their statement here: ﴾If Allah had willed, we would not have associated partners (with Him), nor would our forefathers﴿ because they attributed what is their own will to the will of the Exalted Allah. Nevertheless, He has affirmed His will in His statement:

﴾And if Allah had willed, they would not have associated anything with Him﴿ ﴾6:107﴿. This is the will of creating minds and establishing the system of the community, and the will of organizing the universe with a system that is based on giving the servants the choice in the realm of the actions for which they will be held accountable. If He had willed, He could have decreed another system.

This will is a hidden will that the minds of humans cannot grasp its essence; therefore, Allah reproached them for relying on it while being ignorant of its nature, saying: ﴾Thus did those before them deny﴿. He likened their denial to the denial of those who came before them, thus alluding to the fact that the aim of the polytheists with this argument was to deny the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him). We have previously explained this meaning in this surah (107) at His saying: ﴾And if Allah had willed, they would not have associated partners﴿.
