Because with its straightness, one is closer to the intended destination than with anything else, so the traveler does not go astray in it, nor does he hesitate or become confused.

The path is one, while the ways are many and diverse:

﴾And indeed, this is My straight path, so follow it; and do not follow other paths, for they will separate you from His path. This He has enjoined upon you that you may become righteous. (153)﴿ Al-An'am. The imams narrated from Abu Huraira that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "What I have commanded you, take it; and what I have forbidden you, refrain from it." Ibn Majah and others narrated from al-Irbad ibn Sariya that he said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) gave us a sermon that caused our eyes to shed tears and our hearts to tremble. We said: O Messenger of Allah, this is a farewell sermon; what do you advise us? He said: "I have left you on the clear path, its night is like its day; none will deviate from it after me except one who is doomed. Whoever among you lives will see much disagreement, so adhere to what you know of my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs after me; hold on to it with your molar teeth. And beware of newly invented matters, for every innovation is misguidance. And you must obey, even if a slave from Ethiopia leads you, for indeed, the believer is like a well-trained camel; wherever it is tied, it submits." It was narrated by al-Tirmidhi in a similar manner and authenticated.

Abu Dawood narrated that Ibn Kathir said: Sufyan informed us that a man wrote to Umar ibn Abdul Aziz asking him about predestination (qadar); so he wrote to him: "To proceed, I advise you to fear Allah and to be moderate in your affairs, to follow the Sunnah of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), and to abandon what the innovators have introduced after his Sunnah was established. And you should avoid its burdens. Therefore, adhere to the community, for it is, by the permission of Allah, a protection for you. Then know that no one has innovated an innovation except that there was a precedent for it, either as evidence or as a lesson; for the Sunnah was established by one who knew what was erroneous and misguided in its opposition, and the foolishness and excessiveness therein. Be content with what the people were content with for themselves, for they stood upon knowledge, and with keen insight they were restrained. They were stronger in uncovering matters, and more deserving of the virtue they possessed. If guidance is what you are upon, then you have preceded them to it. And if you say that what occurred happened after them, then what was innovated was only by one who followed a path other than theirs and turned away from them; for they are the forerunners. They spoke about it sufficiently and described what was healing. Those below them are deficient, and those above them are excessive. And indeed, some people fell short of them and became harsh, while others exceeded them and became extreme. Yet, despite that, they are upon a straight guidance." And he mentioned the hadith.

Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal said: Al-Aswad ibn Amer Shadhan narrated to us, Abu Bakr, who is Ibn Ayyash, narrated from Asim, who is Ibn Abi al-Najud, from Abu Wail, from Abdullah, who is Ibn Mas'ud (may Allah be pleased with him): He said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) drew a line with his hand, then said: "This is the straight path of Allah." He drew lines to his right and left, then said: "These are the paths, and there is not one of them except that there is a devil calling to it."
