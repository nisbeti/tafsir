The meaning of worship:

Worship (ibadah) in language means submission and humility towards others with the intention of glorifying them, and it is not permissible to do so except for Allah, and it is used in the meaning of obedience.

Ibn Manzur said: "The servant (abd): a human being, whether free or enslaved, which indicates that he is a created being under his Creator, Exalted is He. It is said that so-and-so is a clear servant of servitude. The essence of servitude is submission and humility. And worship (ibadah) is obedience; Ibn al-Anbari said: 'So-and-so is a worshipper (abid): he is the one who is submissive to his Lord, yielding and compliant to His command.'" ((133)).

Al-Firuzabadi said, "... and worship is obedience" ((134)), and based on this, the definition of worship in the Arabic language is: humiliation and submission that necessitates obedience to the one worshiped in command and prohibition. Therefore, a slave is called "abd" (عبد) because he humbles himself and submits to his master in command and prohibition regarding matters of life.

In terminology: they mentioned several similar definitions for it, including:

(1) - It is the highest rank of submission to Allah and humility before Him.

(2) - She is the one who is obligated against the desires of her own self, in reverence for her Lord.

(3) - It is an action that is intended only to glorify Allah through His command.

(4) - It is a term for what Allah loves and is pleased with among statements, actions, and both outward and inward deeds.

(5) Ibn Kathir (may Allah have mercy on him) defined it as: "The perfection of love for Allah along with the perfection of submission to Allah along with the perfection of fear of Allah."
