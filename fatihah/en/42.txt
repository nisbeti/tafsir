It is upon the person to rectify the condition of the individual, as it is a mercy for him(126). The essence of the sentence and the description is that whoever is characterized by lordship and mercy towards the created beings is deserving of praise(127).
If we reflect, we will find that in the phrase of basmala ﴾In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful﴿, the Benefactor (the One who bestows blessings) is mentioned, but the beneficiary (the one who receives blessings) is not mentioned. Whereas in the surah, Allah mentions the name ﴾The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful﴿ after His saying: ﴾Lord of the worlds﴿; He is Most Gracious to the worlds and Most Merciful to them.
And a similar mention is made by Him, Exalted is He, regarding the straight path (sirat) in the Opening (Al-Fatiha), beginning with those who will walk upon it, or those who will take it as a straight path to follow in this worldly life, as He said: ﴾Guide us to the straight path﴿, then He followed it by mentioning those who will walk upon it and follow the guidance in the methodologies of life: ﴾the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor﴿.
﴾Master of the Day of Judgment﴿
Some of the reciters read ﴾Master of the Day of Judgment﴿ as "the King" which refers to the overwhelming authority, the complete dominance, and the ability to exercise total control over public affairs through command and prohibition, reward and punishment, recompense and accountability, resolving disputes, and rectifying shortcomings. It was also read as ﴾Owner of the Day of Judgment﴿, and both readings are correct and widely transmitted in the seven readings.
And "Malik" (ملك) is derived from "mulk" (ملک), as He said: ﴾Indeed, We will inherit the earth and whoever is upon it, and to Us they will be returned﴿. And He said: ﴾Say, "I seek refuge with the Lord of mankind, * The King of mankind"﴿. And "Malik" (ملك) is taken from "mulk" (ملک), as He, Exalted is He, said: ﴾To whom belongs the dominion today? To Allah, the One, the Prevailing﴿. And He said: ﴾His word is the truth, and to Him belongs the dominion﴿. And He said: ﴾The dominion on that Day is the truth for the Most Merciful, and it will be a difficult Day for the disbelievers﴿.
Ibn Faris said: The letters mīm, lām, and kāf form a correct root that indicates strength in something and soundness. It is said: "I strengthened (amalaka) the dough: I made its mixing strong and firm." And "I empowered (mallektu) the thing: I made it strong. He said:
He possessed with the strength that is above its shell, like the drowning of eggs that the heat has caused from the origin of this. Then it was said that a person possesses something, he owns it with ownership. The name for ownership is "malik"; because his hand in it is strong and sound. Thus, ownership (milk) is what is owned from wealth. The owned (mamluk) is the servant.(128)
