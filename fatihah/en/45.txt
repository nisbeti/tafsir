The reality of worship: it is the ultimate sanctification of the worshipped, Exalted is He, and it is the greatest manifestation of gratitude that the created being must perform towards the One who has bestowed upon him the blessing of creation and existence. It is accomplished through the submission of the heart and limbs to Allah, acting in accordance with what He has commanded, through actions intended solely for the sake of Allah, without any partner, in the manner that the worshipped, Exalted is He, has commanded, out of love and submission to Him, and out of fear of His punishment. He alone is worthy of worship, without any others.
The rulings of worship are definitive
The rulings of worship are definitive from Allah, Exalted is He, and a person performs them in the manner that Allah has commanded, as worship is merely a system that organizes the relationship between the created being and the Creator, that is, the relationship between the worshipper and the worshipped. This system cannot be established by the created being at all, because the created being does not comprehend the reality of the Creator to organize his relationship with Him, nor does he grasp His essence to know how to worship Him. It is impossible for a person to devise a system of worship in his mind that organizes his relationship with the Creator, that is, organizes his sanctification of the Creator, because establishing this organization requires understanding the reality of the Creator, which is impossible. Thus, it has become impossible for a person to formulate the rulings of worship in his mind. Hence, it is necessary for the system of worship to come from the Creator, not from the created being, that is, to come from the worshipped, not from the worshipper. Therefore, the rulings of worship must come solely from Allah, and not from humans, and humans have no role in any part of it, no matter how small, because it is impossible for them to establish it. This system must be conveyed by the Creator to the created being, so that he may worship the deity accordingly. Thus, the need for messengers to convey to people the rulings of worship is an absolute necessity, as it is impossible for people to establish rulings in worship, and because they can only come from Allah, Exalted is He. (135)
Worship can only be derived from revelation:
The purpose of worship is to refine the soul by turning to Allah, submitting to Him, and adhering to His rulings by complying with His commands. It can only be derived through revelation of both types: the Glorious Book and the Sunnah of the infallible Prophet, who does not speak out of desire. Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾And he does not speak out of desire. It is but a revelation revealed﴿.
The condition of intention in worship:
There is no disagreement among the jurists regarding the necessity of intention in worship due to the hadith "Actions are judged by intentions." The wisdom behind the obligation of intention in worship is to distinguish worship from habit, and to differentiate the ranks of some acts of worship from others.
