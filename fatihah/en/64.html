And He who purifies it (8) has succeeded. And he who instills it (10) has failed. ﴿ He is the one who possesses the attributes of guidance, so he is guided, and he possesses the attributes of misguidance, so he goes astray. If he possesses the attributes of misguidance, such as disbelief or committing sins and prohibitions, then these attributes contradict guidance, and he will not be guided and cannot be guided. However, if he possesses the attributes of guidance, such as being guided to Islam and believing in it, following its commands, and avoiding its prohibitions, then these attributes contradict misguidance, and he must be guided, and he must continue on the path of guidance. But if he deviates from the path of guidance by adopting some attributes of misguidance, then he will go astray and will not be guided unless he abandons that action which led him to misguidance.

And based on this, the verses came to define the meaning of divine guidance by clarifying the causes of guidance for humans and indicating what contradicts this guidance in terms of attributes. Allah, Exalted is He, said ﴾How can Allah guide a people who disbelieved after their faith and bore witness that the Messenger is true and came to them the clear signs? And Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people (86)﴿. And He said ﴾And those who were given knowledge see that what has been revealed to you from your Lord is the truth and guides to the path of the Exalted in Might, the Praiseworthy (6)﴿. And He said ﴾And Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people﴿ ﴾And Allah does not guide the disbelieving people﴿ ﴾And Allah does not guide the immoral people﴿ ﴾And indeed, Allah does not guide the plot of the treacherous﴿ ﴾So indeed, Allah does not guide whom He misleads﴿ ﴾Indeed, Allah does not guide whom He knows to be a lying, ungrateful person﴿ ﴾Indeed, Allah does not guide whom He knows to be a wasteful, deceitful person﴿ ﴾Allah chooses for Himself whom He wills and guides to Himself whoever turns back (to Him)﴿. These verses provide conclusive evidence that Allah guides through the Qur'an, for whoever follows the Qur'an and believes in it, He guides him. And whoever turns to Allah or seeks refuge in Him, then Allah guides him or he is on the right path. As for those who possess attributes that contradict guidance, such as the wrongdoer, the immoral, the disbeliever, the misguided, the treacherous, and the wasteful liar, they do not find guidance, meaning that Allah does not grant success in guidance to those who possess these attributes. This is because success in guidance is the preparation of its causes for the person, and whoever possesses these attributes has not had the causes of guidance prepared for him, but rather the causes of misguidance. Thus, a person must tread the path of guidance, and then Allah will grant him success by preparing for him the means that assist him in it.

Al-Shahrani (may Allah have mercy on him) said about the guidance of Allah: "There are two types of guidance: guidance of indication and guidance of assistance. The guidance of indication is for all people... and the guidance of assistance is only for the believers who follow the path of Allah. And Allah, Exalted is He, has guided all His servants with the guidance of indication, meaning He has shown them the path of goodness and made it clear to them... So whoever wants to follow the path of goodness, he follows it... and whoever does not want to follow it, Allah leaves him to what he desires..."

This general guidance is the foundation of conveying the message from Allah. Allah, Exalted is He, has clarified for us in His methodology what actions please Him and what actions anger Him. He has made clear to us the path we should follow to be rightly guided. It is the path that, if we were to tread upon it, would incur Allah's wrath and displeasure.
