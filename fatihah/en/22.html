The meaning of "Allah" is the deity who is worshipped rightfully, whom the creatures worship, and to whom they turn with love, reverence, and submission, seeking refuge in Him for their needs and calamities, due to His attributes of divinity, which are attributes of perfection.(66)

Imam Abu Hamid al-Ghazali said: As for His name (Allah), it is the name of the true existent that encompasses the attributes of divinity, described by the attributes of lordship, unique in true existence, for every existent other than Him does not deserve existence by itself, but rather derives existence from Him,... and it is more appropriate that it runs in its indication on the meaning in the same manner as proper names, and everything mentioned in its derivation and definition is forced and contrived(67)

And what we lean towards is that the essence of the word of Allah revolves around worship, for He is the one worthy of worship (ma'bud) in truth, and it is difficult to resolve the disagreement regarding the origin of the word and whether it is derived or not.
