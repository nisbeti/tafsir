The ones who have incurred wrath are those who recognized their Lord and then deviated from the truth, and they are farthest away because knowledge is not like ignorance. Thus, He began with those who have incurred wrath. In the authentic hadith, it is stated that those who have incurred wrath are the Jews, while the Christians are the misguided. The Jews preceded the Christians, which is why they were mentioned first, necessitating this order.

The characteristic of those who have incurred wrath is the first sin that appeared in existence, which is the characteristic of Iblis (Satan) when he was commanded to prostrate to Adam (peace be upon him) while knowing the truth, yet he disobeyed Allah, Exalted is He. It is also the first sin that appeared on earth when the son of Adam killed his brother. Thus, it is the first sin in the higher realm and on earth ﴾And whoever kills a believer intentionally, his recompense is Hell, wherein he will abide eternally, and Allah will be angry with him and curse him and prepare for him a great punishment (93)﴿ (An-Nisa). Therefore, it was mentioned first.

As for placing those who have incurred wrath alongside those who have been blessed, it is because those who have incurred wrath are the opposite of those who have been blessed, and wrath is the opposite of blessings.

Who among us, no matter how eloquent and articulate, can produce words with such precision and balance, with beautiful structure and careful selection? Every word is a precious gem, meticulously chosen to convey profound meanings with simple words. Each one is a link in a chain that cannot be replaced, moved, brought forward, or delayed; otherwise, the meaning would be distorted, weakened, or some of its meanings would be lost.

It is the divine miracle that manifests throughout the universe and encompasses the entire Qur'an, in its entirety and in its details, its words and letters, its meanings and secrets, to be the light that Allah intended to guide and bring happiness to everyone who has a heart or listens attentively while being present (153).

And praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.

Those who have incurred wrath and the misguided include the factions of disbelief, immorality, and disobedience. The ones who have incurred wrath are a category of factions that deliberately took this path and disregarded the religion either intentionally or through a very far-fetched interpretation, while the misguided are a category of factions that misunderstood the religion due to poor comprehension and lack of attention. Both groups are blameworthy because we are commanded to follow the path of truth and direct our efforts towards achieving it. The Jews belong to the first group, and the Christians belong to the second group. What has been reported in the narration that seemingly interprets those who have incurred wrath as the Jews and the misguided as the Christians indicates that the verse alludes to these two groups that have been characterized by these descriptions, as each has become a sign of what is intended to be alluded to (154).
